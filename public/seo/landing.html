How It Works

Three Steps. Fully Automated.

Pick your targets. The rest runs itself.

1

Scout

Pick a city and business type. Our system pulls hundreds of leads: business names, emails, websites, phone numbers, and ratings. All in seconds.

2

Write

AI reads each lead's website and writes a cold email from scratch, tailored to that specific business, their industry, and what you offer. Not a template with a name swapped in.

3

Send

Emails go out automatically. Opens, clicks, and replies are tracked. If they don't respond, follow-up sequences run on autopilot until they do.
